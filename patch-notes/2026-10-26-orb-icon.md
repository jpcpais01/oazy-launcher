### A new Oazy icon
- **Oazy's icon is now the water orb** from the startup and restart screens, with the Oazy mark floating in it, on the app, the window and in Settings.
- **The Start button is alive.** Its water turns gently, stirs when you hover or open Start, and sends a little ripple out when you click.
- The old square icon is gone from the startup screen.
