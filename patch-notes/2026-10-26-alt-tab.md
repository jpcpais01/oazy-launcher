### Oazy's own Alt + Tab
- **Alt + Tab now opens Oazy's switcher**, a pane of glass with a live preview of every open window, Oazy's own windows included.
- Tab and Shift + Tab or the arrow keys move, Delete closes a window, Esc backs out, and letting go of Alt switches. You can also click a preview.
- A quick tap still jumps straight back to your last window.
- Windows' own switcher still appears over fullscreen games and apps running as administrator. Turn it off any time in Settings.
