### Updates live in a little pill
- While Oazy has an update going, a small glass pill sits on the right edge of the screen, above every window, with the water orb filling as it downloads.
- Hover it to slide open the details: the version, download speed and time left, and one button to update, restart or try again. Click it for the full update screen.
- It opens by itself for a moment when an update is found or finished downloading, and steps aside for fullscreen games.
- The update button on the taskbar is gone.
