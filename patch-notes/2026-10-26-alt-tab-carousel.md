### A new look for Alt + Tab
- **Your windows glide in as a carousel.** The screen sinks into a soft dusk and the window you're about to switch to sits large in the middle on a pool of light, with the others easing off to the sides.
- **Live previews move with their cards** as you Tab through, instead of popping in once everything has stopped.
- The window's name sits underneath in big, clear type, with a row of dots showing where you are.
- Scroll with the mouse to move through your windows, click one to switch, or click anywhere else to back out.
