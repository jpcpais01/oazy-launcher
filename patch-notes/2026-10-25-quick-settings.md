### Quick settings, a calendar and a shortcuts card
- **Sound on the taskbar.** Click the speaker next to the clock for quick settings: a volume slider, mute, and play/pause, next and previous for whatever's playing.
- **Scroll over the speaker** to turn the volume up or down, or middle-click it to mute. The icon shows when you're muted.
- **One click to Windows settings.** Quick settings has tiles for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Display, Night light, Sound and all settings, plus your battery level on laptops.
- **Click the clock for a calendar.** It shows today and lets you flip between months.
- **Press F1** (or right-click the desktop, or search "shortcuts") to see every keyboard shortcut in one place.
