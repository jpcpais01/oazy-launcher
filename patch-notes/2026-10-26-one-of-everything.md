### One of everything

- A game is now the same game everywhere. Its Start shortcut, its window on the taskbar and its Library tile are one thing, with one icon (its cover), one favourite and one pin. No more two different icons for the same game in Favourites.
- Favourites you added before from Start or the taskbar join up with the Library's heart automatically, and duplicates disappear.
- Apps work the same way: an app in your Library shows once in Start and Find apps, and its window groups with its pin.
- Right-click menus match everywhere (desktop, Start, taskbar and Library): Play or Open, Details, Favourite, Pin, then Edit, Show in folder and Add to / Remove from Library, always in that order.
- Games on the taskbar say Play, not New window.
- Removing a game or app from your Library hands its pin and favourite back to its Start app when there is one.
