### Always the newest update
- **Check again any time.** Even when an update is waiting, or already downloaded, Settings has a **Check again** button that looks for an even newer version.
- Oazy now checks quietly every hour, and again whenever you open the update screen, so you always install the latest release instead of an older one.
- If a newer version turns up while the update screen is open, its notes refresh by themselves. Finding the same update again won't pop up another notification.
- The Oazy icon no longer has bubbles in it either, on the app, in Explorer or anywhere else.
