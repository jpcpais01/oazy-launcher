### Cleaner: keep Windows lean, fast and private
- **A new Oazy app** (Start, or search "clean") that looks your PC over and shows what's worth changing: preinstalled apps you never asked for, startup apps that slow sign-in, junk files, and settings that send your data away or show you ads.
- **Nothing changes until you press Apply.** Recommended picks are ticked for you; tap any row to change your mind.
- **Startup apps** get a simple on/off switch. Game launchers can wait, since Oazy starts them when you play.
- **Speed-ups for games**: Game Mode, no background recording, no startup delay, snappier menus and (on desktops) the high performance power plan.
- **Every change can be undone** from History, and Oazy makes a Windows restore point first. Windows asks for permission once per cleanup.
- **Never touched**: Defender, Windows Update, drivers, the Store and anything Windows needs to run.
- **Keep watch**: a couple of minutes after Oazy starts, it checks whether Windows quietly brought anything back, and tells you.
