### A trading desk on your desktop
- Scroll down twice from the desktop to find a new third page: your own crypto trading desk, live from Hyperliquid, in the same glass look as the other pages.
- The coins you follow sit along the top with their price, 24h change and the day's line. Click one to put it on the big candlestick chart. The chart has 5m to 1D candles, volume, and a crosshair that shows each candle's open, high, low and close.
- Next to the chart are the live order book (with depth bars and the spread) and the latest trades as they happen.
- Paste your Hyperliquid wallet address to see your open positions, live P&L, account value and margin. The address is public, so Oazy never asks for a key. If you paste a private key by mistake, Oazy wipes it straight away. You can also set the address in Settings > Desktop & taskbar.
- Prices and your positions keep updating in the background. The order book and trades only stream while you're on the page.
- The page is view-only for now. Trading will come later.
