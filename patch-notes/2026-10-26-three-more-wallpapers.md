### Three more wallpapers

- **Ripple**: slow rings spreading across still water.
- **Bloom**: long petals of tinted glass fanning in from the corner, swaying gently.
- **Shallows**: sunlight falling in shafts through clear water, with light dancing on the sand below.
- Find them in **Settings**, under **Wallpaper**. Like the others they follow the wallpaper light and your accent colour.
