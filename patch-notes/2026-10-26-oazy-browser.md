### Oazy Browser
- **Oazy has its own web browser.** It's Chromium, the engine inside Chrome and Edge, running in an Oazy glass window, so every site works like it does in Chrome. Find it on your taskbar next to Library, or search "Browser" in Start.
- **Tabs that feel right**: drag to reorder, middle-click to close, right-click to duplicate, mute or move a tab to its own window. A speaker shows which tab is playing sound.
- **One bar for everything**: type an address or a search and get suggestions from your search engine and the sites you've been to, with the rest of an address filled in as you type. Pick Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo or Brave from the menu.
- **A calm new tab page** on the glass: the time, a search box and your most visited sites. Keep your favourites there with the star in the address bar.
- **Downloads** show their progress right on the toolbar, with a list where you can pause, resume, open a file or show it in its folder.
- **History, find on page (Ctrl+F), zoom, full screen (F11), print** and all the usual shortcuts: Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+Shift+T, Ctrl+L, Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+1–9.
- **It remembers you**: sign-ins, cookies and your open tabs are still there next time. Closing or updating Oazy never closes the browser.
- Web searches and addresses typed in Start now open in Oazy Browser. Prefer your usual browser? Switch it off in Settings.
