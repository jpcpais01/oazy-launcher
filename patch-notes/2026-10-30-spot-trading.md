### Spot trading on the trading desk
- A small **Perps / Spot** toggle at the top right switches the whole desk between perpetuals and spot markets.
- Spot keeps its own saved tickers and chart coin, so each mode remembers its own list.
- In spot the Trade box is a plain buy or sell: no leverage, TP/SL or reduce-only, and it shows what you get and what it uses.
- The bottom panel shows your token balances and spot orders, with spot value, tokens held and free USDC.
