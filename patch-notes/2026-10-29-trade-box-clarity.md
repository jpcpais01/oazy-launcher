### Clearer Trade box, more chart

- **Your money at a glance**: the Trade box opens with your account value and one bar showing what's in use, what the order you're typing would take, and what's left free.
- **The order in plain words**: under the form, what you get, how much margin and fee it uses, and what TP / SL would make or lose. It warns you if the order needs more than you have free.
- **Tidier form**: Market / Limit sit on the size row, and TP / SL boxes are labelled inside.
- **More chart**: the mark / oracle / volume / funding row is gone (hover the big price to see it), and the O H L C readout only shows while you hover the chart.
