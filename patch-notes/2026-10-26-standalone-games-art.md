### League of Legends and other non-Steam games, with real art

- Games installed outside the big stores now show up too: things like Genshin Impact, Wargaming, Rockstar or other publishers' own installers, and anything living in a **Games** folder.
- **League of Legends**, **VALORANT**, Ubisoft and Amazon games now get proper covers, backgrounds and logos from GOG Galaxy's game database.
- Games that only have a cover now use it for their big background instead of a colour pattern.
