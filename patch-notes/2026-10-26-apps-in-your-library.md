### Apps in your Library
- **Right-click any app** in Start, search, the taskbar or on your desktop and choose **Add to Library**. Library apps get **Remove from Library** in the same menus.
- **Find apps** in the Library lists everything installed on your PC with its icon. Tick the ones you want and add them all at once.
- Added apps get a cover: Oazy's own artwork for popular apps, or the app's icon on a matching tint for the rest.
- An app keeps its taskbar pin, favourite and open windows when it moves into the Library, and gets them back if you remove it.
