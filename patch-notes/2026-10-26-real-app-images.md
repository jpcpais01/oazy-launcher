### Real images for your apps

- WhatsApp, Higgsfield, Discord, Flow, Claude, Spotify, Chrome, VS Code and about 30 more apps now use their **official artwork**: the image each app's own website shows when it's shared. It becomes the big background, and a framed poster version becomes the cover.
- Oazy downloads it once and keeps it on your PC. Offline, or if a site has no image, Oazy's built-in art still shows.
