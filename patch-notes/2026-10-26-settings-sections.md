### Settings, reorganised

- Settings now has a sidebar with clear sections: Personalise, Desktop & taskbar, Apps & library, Browser & terminal, Network, Performance, Updates and About.
- Find a setting: type in the search box and every matching setting shows up, from any section.
- New in Settings: weather units (Auto, °C, °F), a Network page with your connection and the Wi-Fi switch, Cleaner's Keep watch, and quick buttons to open the Library, Cleaner, Oazy Browser, Oazy Terminal and the keyboard shortcuts.
- Your Oazy version and update status sit at the bottom of the sidebar, one click from Updates.
