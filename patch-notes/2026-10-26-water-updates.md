### Updates and startup, reimagined
- **Updates pour in.** Downloads now fill a glass orb with water, with live speed, time left and Download, Verify, Restart steps.
- **A smooth restart.** When the update is verified a ripple runs out, and Oazy settles into its startup screen so the restart feels like one move.
- **Welcome back.** After an update, the new version number rolls into place and what's new shows up as clear cards, one per change.
- **Faster, prettier startup.** Oazy opens with the same water orb, and gets you to your desktop sooner.
