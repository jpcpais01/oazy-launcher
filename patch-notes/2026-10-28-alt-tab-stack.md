### Alt+Tab as a 3D stack
- Alt+Tab now shows your windows as a tilted stack of glass cards leaning back into the screen, the chosen one at the front. Tab sends the front card to the back of the stack; Shift+Tab brings the last one round to the front.
- Click any card in the stack to switch to it, middle-click to close it, scroll to cycle.
- Minimised windows show what was in them, in Alt+Tab and in the taskbar's hover preview, instead of just their icon. Oazy's own windows show a real picture too.
