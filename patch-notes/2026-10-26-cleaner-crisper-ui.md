### A cleaner, crisper Oazy
- **Lighter glass**: thinner edges, a quieter rim and softer shadows on every window, popup and panel.
- **Library refined**: flatter sidebar with a hairline divider, slimmer search and buttons, calmer covers with a thin focus ring instead of a coloured glow.
- **Swifter motion**: windows, Start, dialogs, buttons and cards open and react faster, with smooth eased moves instead of springy bounces.
- Headlines are a touch lighter and tighter across Library, Settings, Cleaner and updates.
