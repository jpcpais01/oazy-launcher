### Coin logos and a Today chart

- **Real logos**: coins and stocks on the trading desk now show their logos, straight from Hyperliquid (stocks and ETFs too). If Hyperliquid has none, Oazy tries a second source, and falls back to the coloured letter.
- **Today**: stocks, ETFs and indices get a **Today** button on the chart. It shows the market from the first candle of the US trading day (9:30 New York time, summer time handled). Before the open, and on weekends, it shows the last trading day.
