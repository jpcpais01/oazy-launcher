### Favourites on your desktop

- Your favourites now sit on the desktop under the clock, straight on the wallpaper, lined up with Recently played. Right-click any app or game and choose Add to favourites.
- Processor, graphics and memory are now one slim strip under the date. Hover any of them to see clock speeds and temperatures.
- Fixed File Explorer and Store apps like WhatsApp showing twice on the taskbar when they were open.
