### Windows that open right the first time
- Browser, Files, Terminal, Claude and Flow open exactly as you left them, maximized or not, without needing a restore-then-maximize to look right.
- The dark band along the top of Oazy's own windows is gone for good: Oazy's dark title colouring for other apps no longer touches its own glass windows.
- The taskbar now docks flush under a maximized Oazy window, the same as under any other maximized app.
- Clicking any visible part of a window behind another one brings it to the front.
