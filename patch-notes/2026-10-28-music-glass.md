### Music wallpaper glass
- The bars are now clear, squared glass columns, a little wider, and the row reaches almost to the screen edges with smaller columns at the sides. Light passes through each column's flat face and bends through its bevelled edges, so the edges only shine where real light sits beside them. The drawn white outline is gone.
- The song title and play bar now scroll away with the desktop's first screen, so they no longer draw over the markets, news and weather below.
- The timeline is a plain hairline again, without the little waveform around the playhead.
