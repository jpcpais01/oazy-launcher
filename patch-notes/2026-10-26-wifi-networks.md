### Wi-Fi right from the taskbar

- The screen icon in the tray is now a **network button**: it shows your Wi-Fi strength (or a cable when you're on Ethernet). Show desktop lives on the pill at the far right and the screen corner.
- Click it for the networks around you, strongest first, and join one right there: type the key in place, choose whether to connect automatically, or **Forget** a saved network.
- See what you're on at a glance: network name, internet or not, 2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz band and link speed, plus your Ethernet cable when one is plugged in.
- **Disconnect** with one click, turn Wi-Fi on or off from the switch, and press refresh to look again.
- Wrong key? Oazy says what Windows said and asks again, without keeping the bad key.
