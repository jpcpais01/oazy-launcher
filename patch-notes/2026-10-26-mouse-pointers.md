### Oazy mouse pointers
- **Ten new pointer themes**: Oazy, Snow, Ink, Aero, Liquid glass, Lagoon, Ember, Orchid, Graphite and Aurora. Each is a complete set: arrow, link hand, text, busy and working spinners (animated), precision, unavailable, help, move and every resize arrow.
- **They follow you into every app** while Oazy is running, not just Oazy's own windows. Close Oazy and Windows' own pointers come straight back, even if Oazy didn't shut down cleanly.
- Pick one in **Settings → Appearance → Mouse pointer**, with a live preview of each. **Off** keeps Windows' default.
- Sharp at any display scale, and they respect the pointer size you chose in Windows.
