### Party mode for the music wallpaper
- While the music wallpaper is on, a small star slides out beside its taskbar button. Click it to turn the music into a celebration.
- The glass bars now stand on a still lake at dusk, lit warm from behind, while fine fireworks open in the sky on the beat and soft beams sway behind the row.
- Golden glints drift up into the night and the lake mirrors everything, rippling gently. The colours still follow the song's vibe.
- Party mode is also in the music button's right-click menu, and it switches off with the music wallpaper.
