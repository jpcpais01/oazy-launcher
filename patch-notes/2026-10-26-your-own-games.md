### Your own games, found automatically

- Games you make with **Godot** are recognised on their own. Put the exported .exe on your Desktop, in Documents or Downloads, or in a **Games** folder.
- Anything in a **Games** folder (like `C:\Games` or `D:\Games\My Game`) counts as a game.
- Give a game its own art by putting images next to its .exe: **cover** for the tile, **banner** (or hero) for the big background, **logo** for the title. Plain names work (`banner.png`), and so do names with the game in front, like `My Game_banner.png` or `my-game-cover.jpg`.
- Removed a game Oazy found by itself? It now stays removed.
