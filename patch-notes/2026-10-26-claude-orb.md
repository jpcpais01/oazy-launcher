### The orb is Claude now
- Click the orb on the taskbar and Claude rises out of it: a small glass chat right above the taskbar. Ask anything, or start from one of the suggestions.
- Replies stream in as Claude writes them, with proper lists, tables and code. While it works, a little water orb stirs, and so does the taskbar orb, even with the chat closed.
- It's the Claude Code on your PC, signed in as you, starting in your home folder. It can look around freely, but anything that changes a file or runs a command waits for your **Allow**.
- **New chat** starts fresh, and **Open in Claude** carries the conversation over to the full Claude app.
- Start is still one click away: click the search bar or right-click the orb. **Ctrl + Space** opens Claude.
