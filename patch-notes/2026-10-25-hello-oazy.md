### Hello, Oazy
- **Your games, one place.** Oazy finds your Steam, Epic Games and GOG games by itself the first time you open it, with their covers, backgrounds and logos.
- **A home screen that feels like a console.** Browse with the arrow keys, mouse wheel or a controller; the whole screen takes on the colours and artwork of the game you're looking at.
- **Add anything.** Any program, shortcut, Steam link or website. Drop a file onto the window, or use **+ Add**.
- **Library** with instant search (just start typing), filters for games, apps and favourites, and sorting by recent, A–Z or most played.
- **Playtime tracking** for the games you launch from Oazy.
- **Updates from inside the app**: see what's new, watch the download, and Oazy restarts into the new version by itself.
- Soft interface sounds, an Eco mode for laptops, start with Windows, fullscreen with F11, and more in Settings.
