### Three new wallpapers

- **Aurora**: silk ribbons of light drifting across a deep sky.
- **Tide**: layered sheets of liquid glass, each with a softly lit crest.
- **Drift**: a few large glass bubbles floating in soft light.
- Pick one in **Settings → Appearance → Wallpaper**, from live previews. Oasis is still there, and stays the default.
- Every wallpaper follows the wallpaper light (dawn, day, dusk, night) and your accent colour, and crossfades when you switch.
- The wallpaper no longer gives a tiny jump once an hour.
