### Weather on your taskbar
- **The weather now sits next to the clock**: a colour icon and the temperature where you are. Nothing to set up; Oazy works out your town by itself.
- **Click it for the forecast**: feels like, humidity and wind, the next twelve hours, and the week ahead with each day's low and high drawn against the whole week.
- Switch between **°C and °F** from the forecast card (it picks the right one for your country to start with).
- Updates every half hour. Offline, it keeps showing the forecast it already has, and quietly tries again a few minutes later.
