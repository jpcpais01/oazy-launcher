### Windows that do what you expect
- **Apps you open come to the front, ready to type in,** even when you start them from the taskbar. If you move on to another window while one is still loading, it opens behind without stealing focus, like on Windows.
- **Clicking a window always brings it forward with the keyboard,** whether it's another app, one of Oazy's windows or a dialog (a Save box comes up with its app). Closing Start, a menu or a flyout hands focus straight back to the window you were in, and Oazy no longer taps a stray Alt that could open an app's menu bar.
- **No more invisible spots that swallow clicks** beside a taskbar preview or between two of Oazy's windows: clicks there reach the window underneath.
- **Other apps' title bars are left alone.** Oazy now only tints their borders (Settings, *Tint app window borders*), so no window opens with a stale dark title bar that needed restore and maximize to look right.
- **Taskbar previews stay calm:** sweeping quickly along the taskbar no longer flicks the preview through every app on the way, getting from an icon up to its preview is more forgiving, and the preview's contents never spill out while it glides.
- **Show desktop hides everything,** Oazy's own windows like Library and Settings included, and the next click brings it all back in the same order, with the front window focused again.
