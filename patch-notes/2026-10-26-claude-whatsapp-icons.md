### Claude and WhatsApp icons
- **Claude gets its own Oazy icon**: a warm spark on terracotta glass. Claude Code gets a matching one with a prompt beside the spark, so it no longer looks like any other terminal.
- **WhatsApp gets its own too**: a green bubble with a phone.
- Both are **on straight away**, including if you'd already picked your icons. Turn them off any time under Settings → Appearance → Oazy app icons.
