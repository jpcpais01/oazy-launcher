### A cleaner Alt + Tab
- **The switcher has a calmer, simpler look.** Each window is a plain rounded picture with a soft shadow, the selected one gets a thin white ring, and the screen behind dims evenly, with no coloured glow.
- The window's name sits under the picture, with the app's icon on the card's edge so you can tell windows apart at a glance.
- Motion is quicker and smoother, and the help text is gone to keep it tidy.
