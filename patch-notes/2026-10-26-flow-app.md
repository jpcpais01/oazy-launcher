### Flow, as an Oazy app
- **Google Flow has its own app now.** Find Flow in Start or on the taskbar: it opens in a window of its own with a slim Oazy title bar, no tabs or address bar, and you're already signed in if you are in Oazy Browser.
- **The page wears Oazy's look**: it sits on the window's glass, panels turn to frosted glass, the prompt box becomes a glass dock with an accent Create button, and your images and videos get soft tiles. Google's site still does all the generating underneath.
- **Click the spark in the title bar (or press Ctrl+Shift+O)** to switch the look off and see Flow exactly as Google draws it. If Flow changes and Oazy can't find its way around a page, the spark turns amber and the page simply shows as it is.
- Links to other sites open in Oazy Browser, and anything you download shows up as a note in the title bar; click it to open your Downloads.
