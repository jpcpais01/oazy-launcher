### Search does more
- **Quick sums.** Type `19.99*3`, `2^10` or `sqrt(2)` in Start and the answer appears at the top. Press Enter to copy it.
- **Jump to Windows settings.** Type `wifi`, `bluetooth`, `display`, `sound`, `night light`, `storage` or `uninstall` to open the right Windows settings page.
- **System commands.** `lock`, `sleep`, `restart`, `shut down`, `task manager` and `recycle bin` work straight from search, and so do Oazy's own: check for updates, patch notes, scan for games, add a game.
- **Type an address or a folder.** `github.com` opens in your browser, and `C:\Games` or `~/Downloads` opens in Explorer.
- **Smarter ranking.** Apps you open often and games you played recently now rise to the top of the results.
