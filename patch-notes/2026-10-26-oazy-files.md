### Oazy Files
- **Oazy has its own file explorer.** Your folders open in an Oazy glass window with tabs, a sidebar of Quick access, pinned folders and drives, and a Home view with your recent files. The File Explorer icon on your taskbar opens it, and so do Downloads, Documents and Pictures in Start, folder paths you type in Start and the data folder in Settings.
- **Big, sharp thumbnails** for photos and videos, real app icons, and an icons or details view with sorting and Ctrl+mouse wheel to resize. A details pane shows a preview and the file's size and dates.
- **Everything you expect**: open, rename (F2), new folder, cut, copy and paste (also with other apps), delete to the Recycle Bin or for good, drag and drop between folders, tabs and the sidebar, drag files out to other apps or in from them, Extract here and Compress to ZIP, Open in Terminal, Open with and Properties.
- **Search** the folder you're in and everything below it from the box at the top; results stream in as they're found.
- Picks things out with a drag box, arrow keys and typing the start of a name. Folders refresh by themselves when something changes.
- Prefer Windows' own? Switch "Open folders in Oazy Files" off in Settings.
