### A steadier desktop
- **Apps stop jumping.** Maximised windows used to resize every couple of seconds while Oazy and Windows argued over where the taskbar was. Oazy's taskbar now reserves its space the way Windows expects, so apps fit once and stay put.
- **Getting back to your desktop is one click.** Use the new desktop button on the taskbar, or press **Ctrl + Alt + D** from any app. Do it again to bring your windows back.
- **Start fits again.** The side column (Library, File Explorer, Downloads and the rest) no longer spills off the edge, and your desktop fades away while Start is open, so the clock isn't left cut in half behind it.
- **The Start button is the Oazy icon** instead of the leaf.
- Softer shadows that fade out instead of ending in hard lines around Start, the taskbar, windows and notifications.
