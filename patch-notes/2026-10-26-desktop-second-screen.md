### There's more below your desktop
- **Scroll down** on the desktop (or press Page Down) and it glides to a second screen; scroll up to come back. A faint arrow at the bottom shows the way.
- **Ten covers in a row** for the games and apps you play most from your favourites. Click to launch, right-click for the usual menu.
- **Markets**: live prices with a sparkline of the day. Click the pencil to choose your own stocks, indices, crypto or currencies.
- **News**: the latest headlines, with Top, World, Business, Tech, Games and Science. Click one to read it.
- **The week's weather** and **this month's calendar**, side by side.
- Nothing down there loads or ticks until you scroll to it, and it rests again when you scroll back up.
