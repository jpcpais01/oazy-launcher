### Your apps open inside Oazy
- **Oazy is your desktop now.** Chrome, Claude Code, games and every other app open on Oazy's wallpaper, under Oazy's taskbar. Switching to an app no longer takes you out of Oazy, and the taskbar, Start and previews stay on top the whole time.
- **The Windows taskbar steps aside** while Oazy runs, and maximised apps stop just above Oazy's taskbar. Close Oazy and everything goes back to normal. You can keep the Windows taskbar or turn the whole desktop mode off in **Settings**.
- **Library and Settings are real windows.** They sit in front of or behind your other apps like any window, and **Ctrl + Alt + O** opens Start from anywhere.
- **Fullscreen games and videos get the screen.** Oazy's taskbar hides while something is fullscreen.
- **Other apps match Oazy.** Their title bars and borders take on Oazy's colours on Windows 11 (you can turn this off in Settings).

### Favourites
- **Right-click any app, game or program** (on the taskbar, in Start, on the desktop or in your library) and choose **Add to favourites**.
- Favourites get their own spot **on your desktop** and **at the top of Start**.

### A cleaner look
- A calm new wallpaper of soft colour that still follows the time of day, without the ribbons and bubbles.
- Clearer liquid glass everywhere, with a fine edge of light instead of the glossy shine, and quieter app tiles and covers.
