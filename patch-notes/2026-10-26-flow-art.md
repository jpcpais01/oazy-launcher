### Flow gets its own card
- **Flow now has its own artwork**: a cover, a wide banner and a logo in its violet and blue, in the same style as the rest of Oazy's built-in art.
- **Flow sits in your Library as a card** next to your games and apps, so it shows up with its art in the Library and on the desktop. Its taskbar pin and favourite carry over.
- **Oazy's Claude app can join the Library too**: pick Add to Library from its menu in Start and it gets a card with Claude's art.
