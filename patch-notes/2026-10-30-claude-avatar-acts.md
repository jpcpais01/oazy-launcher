### Claude's avatar comes alive

- **It acts out what you're talking about.** Ask about money and it watches a live chart while coins pile up; physics or maths and it writes on a chalkboard; code and it types on a little laptop in glasses. There are 22 of these, from cooking in a chef's hat and gazing through a telescope to keepy-uppies with a football, watering a plant until it blooms, and popcorn for films.
- **Many more feelings**: heart eyes, star eyes, laughing, shy, confused and determined, with little signs over its head (a question mark, a bulb for ideas, a drop of sweat when things go wrong).
- **It uses its hands now**: it waves hello, holds a magnifier while reading, a hammer while making changes, and rests its chin while thinking.
- **A life of its own when left alone**: it stretches and yawns, whistles, blows bubbles, chases a butterfly, sneezes, dances, does the odd flip, puts on headphones when music plays, and eventually dozes off. Poke it a few times and it gets dizzy.
