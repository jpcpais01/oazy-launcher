### Maximized windows, flush and clean
- Maximized Oazy windows (Browser, Files, Terminal, Claude and Flow) now fill the screen exactly down to the taskbar, with no gap underneath and no black strip along the top edge.
- Drag the title of a maximized window to pull it back to its own size under your pointer; double-click the title, Win+Up or snapping to the top of the screen still maximize as before.
