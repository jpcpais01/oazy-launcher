### Finds all your games, not just Steam and Epic

- Oazy now also picks up games from the **EA app**, **Ubisoft Connect**, **Battle.net**, **Riot Games** (VALORANT, League), **Amazon Games** and the **Xbox app / Game Pass**.
- Games installed in two places show up as one tile, using the best store.
- Uninstalled Epic and GOG games and GOG DLCs no longer appear as games.
- Start's app list hides uninstallers, setup and updater helpers, double shortcuts and games that already have a library tile, while keeping real apps that just happen to have words like "help" in their name.
