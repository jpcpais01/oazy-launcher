### Trading desk v2

- **Your real balance**: total value now counts all your money on Hyperliquid, including unified accounts and spot balances. A new **Balances** tab lists what you hold.
- **Take profit and stop loss**: tick TP / SL on a new order, or set them on any open position with its **TP / SL** button. They show up in Orders and as lines on the chart.
- **A bigger chart**: the book and trades now share one slim panel, the positions bar is shorter, and the chart has much more room. Your entry, liquidation, TP, SL and open orders are drawn right on it.
- **Easier trading**: quick 10% / 25% / 50% / Max sizes, how much you have free to trade, and clicking a price in the book uses it for a limit order. You can also add your wallet straight from the Trade box.
- **Fixes**: the 5m / 15m / 1H / 4H / 1D buttons now highlight the one you picked, and ROE matches Hyperliquid's.
