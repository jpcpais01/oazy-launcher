### Your games are back in the spotlight
- **The big game banner is back**, larger than ever, with a slow drift across the art and a smooth crossfade when you pick another game.
- **Large cover cards** for your games line up under it. Hover for a lift, a glow and a light sweep; click one to show it on the banner, double-click to play. The last card opens your whole Library.
- **The clock is back on the left**, with the date and the weather right under it.
- **Favourites, recent apps and This PC** sit in a tidy column beside your games instead of floating around the screen.
