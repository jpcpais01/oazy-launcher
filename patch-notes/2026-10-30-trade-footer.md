### Tidier Trade box
- The controls under the Trade box sit in two neat rows. The first has the Perps / Spot switch, the coin editor and the Live light. The second shows your API wallet and Remove key.
- Nothing there runs past the box any more, on any screen size, and the Trade box itself always keeps the width its buttons need.
- "Trading on/off" isn't repeated under the box, because the switch at the top of the Trade box already shows it.
