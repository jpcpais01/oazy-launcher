### A cleaner, crisper typeface everywhere

- Oazy now uses one modern typeface, Geist, across the whole shell: desktop, Start, Library, Settings, windows and overlays.
- Browser, Files, Terminal, Claude and the Flow look use the same typeface, so every window matches.
- No more drop shadows under text. A soft, barely visible shade in the wallpaper keeps the desktop clock and labels easy to read.
