### Oazy Browser behaves like the other windows
- Coming back to the Browser from the taskbar or Alt+Tab, your typing goes where it went before (the page or the address bar), instead of nowhere until you click.
- Leaving a video or page fullscreen puts the window back exactly as it was, maximized or at its own size, with the taskbar docking correctly again.
- Maximizing, snapping or dragging the title no longer fights a page that is fullscreen.
- A menu or panel that is open while the window changes size now closes, instead of leaving a stretched picture of the page behind it.
