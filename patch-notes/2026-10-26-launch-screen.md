### A calmer, prettier launch screen
- Starting a game now shows its artwork as a soft, blurred backdrop with the game's logo crisp in front.
- A small water orb fills while the game loads, instead of a bar that finished before the game did.
- Oazy steps aside the moment the game's window appears, with a gentle fade into the game.
- Slow launchers get a quiet "Still starting" note, and Esc takes you back to your desktop while you wait.
- If a game can't start, you'll see why and can jump straight to its details.
