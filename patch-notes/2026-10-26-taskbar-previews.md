### Taskbar previews that follow you
- **One preview that glides from app to app** as you move along the taskbar, instead of cards popping, overlapping or getting mixed up when you sweep across quickly.
- **Getting up to a preview is easy now**: head from the icon towards the card, even diagonally across other icons, and it waits for you. It only switches apps when you actually stop on another icon.
- It closes a moment after you leave the icon and the card, and doesn't pop straight back up after you click an icon.
- The Start orb's hover glow can't get stuck any more either.
