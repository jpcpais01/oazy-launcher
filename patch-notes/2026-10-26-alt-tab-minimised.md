### Alt + Tab shows minimised windows too
- **Minimised windows now show a picture in Alt + Tab** instead of just their icon. Oazy quietly keeps a recent picture of each open window, so there's one ready even after you minimise it.
- The window you're using is refreshed every few seconds and the others now and then, one at a time. Oazy pauses this while a fullscreen game is running.
- A window that was minimised before Oazy started still shows its icon until you open it once.
