### Sign in to Oazy with Google

- **Your profile is your Google account.** Click your name in Start (or open **Settings → Account**) and pick one of the Google accounts already in Chrome on this PC, or use another one. Your name and photo show up in Start, Settings and the desktop's greeting.
- **One sign-in for the web.** Signing in also signs in Oazy Browser, Flow and your other web apps, since they all share one sign-in.
- **Sign-in windows that fit Oazy.** Google's sign-in opens in a small glass window, set to dark so it matches the rest of Oazy, with the site's address always in view. "Sign in with Google" popups from websites open the same way.
- Google still asks for your password or passkey once on its own page. Oazy never sees it, and Chrome stays signed in as it is.
- Sign out from Start or Settings to sign Oazy and its web apps out of Google.
