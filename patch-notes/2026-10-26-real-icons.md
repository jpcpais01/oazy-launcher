### Real icons everywhere

- Games and apps now show their own real icon everywhere icons appear: Favourites, Start, search, the taskbar, Alt+Tab and window title bars, with the same softly rounded corners. A game's icon comes from its exe, its Start shortcut, or the main exe in its install folder.
- Covers stay where covers belong (the Library, Recently played, the hero card). A game's cover only stands in as its icon when it has no icon of its own, such as a web link.
