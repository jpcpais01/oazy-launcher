### Temperatures and clock speeds on This PC
- **Graphics ring**: your GPU's load, named by its model (RTX 4070, RX 7800 XT...), with its clock speed and temperature underneath.
- **Processor** now shows its live clock speed, and **Memory** shows how many GB are in use.
- Your display's **refresh rate** sits in the corner of the card.
- **CPU temperature** is an option in Settings › Your Oazy. Oazy reads it from HWiNFO or LibreHardwareMonitor while one is running, otherwise from your motherboard's sensor. No admin rights or extra drivers needed.
- Anything your PC doesn't report is simply left out.
