### Claude, as an Oazy app
- **A Claude app made for Oazy.** It runs the Claude Code on your PC, signed in with your own Claude account, inside a glass chat window: pick a folder, ask, and watch Claude work.
- **Every step is a card**: files it reads, edits (with a green and red diff), commands it runs and their output, and its to-do list as it goes. Click a card to see the details.
- **You stay in charge.** When Claude wants to edit a file or run a command, it asks right above the message box: **Allow** (Enter), **Deny** (Esc), or tell it what to do instead. Switch between *Ask first*, *Auto-edit* and *Plan first* with Shift+Tab, and pick the model next to it.
- **Your conversations are on the left**, the same ones Claude Code keeps in the terminal. Click one to pick up where you left off, or press **Terminal** to carry the conversation on in Oazy Terminal.
- Paste or drop images into the message box. If Claude Code isn't installed yet, the app installs it for you in one click.
