### Music wallpaper
- A new equaliser button on the taskbar turns the desktop into a music visualiser: a sun resting on a dark oasis, with three sheets of liquid glass along the bottom that are the song's spectrum, rings across the water on every kick, and light that swells with the music.
- It listens to Google Chrome alone, so YouTube Music drives it even while minimised and game or notification sounds don't. With Chrome closed it listens to all sound. Right-click the button to switch, or see Settings, Personalise.
- It reads the song's vibe as it plays: tempo, loudness, how busy and bright it sounds, and its key and whether it's major or minor. Calm songs glow turquoise, joyful ones amber and coral, tense ones violet and magenta, sad ones deep blue, and colours drift as the song changes.
- It stops listening while a game is fullscreen.
