### Music wallpaper, reimagined
- The music wallpaper is now a row of thin glass bars dancing to the song, lit from behind by soft coloured light that spills through them as rays and haze, on a calm celestial sky with drifting nebulae, stars and sparkles. Its colours still follow the song's mood.
- What's playing now shows on the desktop with no box around it: the artist and title on the left, and a fine timeline with a little live waveform at the playhead, plus previous, play/pause and next. Click or drag the timeline to seek. It works with YouTube and YouTube Music in Chrome, and with any app that shows up in Windows' media controls.
- Click the bare desktop to clear everything away but the music and the taskbar. Click again to bring it all back.
