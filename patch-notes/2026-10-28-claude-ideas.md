### Claude on the roof, with ideas
- Claude's jelly avatar now sits on top of the orb popup, outside it, and the tall header is gone. Past chats, new chat and the Claude app moved to the footer.
- **Ideas from Claude:** every 5 to 20 minutes Claude quietly reads one of your GitHub projects (latest version) in the background and has one idea for it. It says it in a little bubble on the roof; click it to hear more. A new one only comes after you've seen the last one.
- New settings gear in the popup: turn ideas on or off, allow everything, and pick which GitHub projects Claude thinks about.
