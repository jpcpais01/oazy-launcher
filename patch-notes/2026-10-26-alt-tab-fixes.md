### Alt + Tab, fixed up
- **Live previews show up.** The cards had been covering Windows' live pictures of your apps. Each card now has a clear window where its preview sits, shaped to the app's own proportions.
- **A deeper dim** behind the switcher, so the app you came from no longer shows through under the titles.
- **Every card fits on screen.** Cards are spaced by their real size, so they don't overlap or run off the edge any more.
- **A close button on every card.** Hover a card, or select it, and click the small × at the top to close that window. Middle-click and Delete still work too.
- Each card now shows the app's icon and window title along the top. Minimised apps get a large icon on a wash of their own colour, and there's a small hint of the keys underneath.
