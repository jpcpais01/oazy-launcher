### Claude has a face now
- The orb's Claude chat has a little jelly Claude living along its top, made of the same water as Oazy's orb. It hops around, blinks, breathes and follows your pointer.
- It reacts to the conversation: it watches you type, reacts to what you send, looks up while thinking, paces while it reads, focuses while it runs things, comes to the middle when it needs your OK, and talks as the reply arrives.
- Claude picks its own mood for each reply (happy, curious, proud, sorry and more), and the face shows it. Click it for a giggle.
