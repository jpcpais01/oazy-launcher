### Oazy is your desktop now
- **A real taskbar.** Everything you have open, Chrome, Claude Code, Discord, your games, shows up at the bottom. Click to switch, click again to minimise, and hover for a live preview of each window.
- **Start, the glowing orb.** Open any installed app, game, folder or website from one place. Pin favourites, browse all apps, or just start typing on the desktop to search.
- **Windows like you're used to.** Library and Settings now open as glass windows you can move, resize, snap to the sides, maximise and minimise, several at once.
- **Come home from anywhere.** Press **Ctrl + Alt + O** in any app to jump back to Oazy.
- **A calmer, brighter look.** Liquid glass panels over a living oasis wallpaper that follows the time of day, with a fresh lagoon accent and a new app icon.
- **Claude Code is found and pinned for you** if it's installed.
- Your desktop greets you with the time, your last game ready to continue, and a quick look at your PC's processor and memory.
