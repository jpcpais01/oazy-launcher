### The launch screen you knew is back
- Starting a game shows the familiar screen again: sharp artwork, the cover, the name and the light bar. The blurred version is gone.
- Its motion is a little smoother, with soft eased movement and no spring.
- It still waits for the game's window before fading away, and steps aside if a launcher pops up a dialog.
- Slow launches and failed launches still get a short note, and **Edit details** when something's wrong.
