### A taskbar that stays put
- **The taskbar no longer goes missing behind your windows.** After a game, a fullscreen video or the lock screen it could come back underneath other apps. Now it always comes back on top.
- **Only a real fullscreen game or video hides it,** and only on the screen Oazy is on. A maximised window on a second monitor, Task View and Alt + Tab leave it alone, as Windows does with its own taskbar.
- **It puts itself right.** Oazy checks every couple of seconds that the taskbar is showing, on top and the right size, so a missed signal, Windows minimising everything, a resolution change or a restart of Oazy's Windows helper can't leave it hidden.
