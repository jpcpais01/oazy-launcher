### A slimmer taskbar
- **Takes far less of your screen.** The taskbar is a thinner glass capsule now, and apps get about 26 more pixels at the bottom.
- **Melts into a shelf when an app is maximised.** Over the desktop it floats. When a window fills the screen it stretches edge to edge so no strip of wallpaper peeks out underneath.
- **A liquid lens follows the active app.** A drop of brighter glass glides and stretches from icon to icon as you switch apps.
- **More Oazy-style icons, your choice.** Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Steam, Discord, VS Code, Spotify, Notepad, Calculator, Settings, Photos, Paint and Task Manager can all get Oazy icons. Pick which ones under Settings → Appearance → Oazy app icons. File Explorer and Terminal stay on by default.
