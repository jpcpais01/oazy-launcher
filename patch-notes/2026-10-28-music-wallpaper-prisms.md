### Music wallpaper, brighter and deeper
- The bars are now fewer, chunkier glass prisms with a lit face, a bright edge and glowing top and bottom faces, each holding several colours at once. The row rises to a tall centre with a hump either side and tapers to tiny bars at the ends.
- A bright core of light burns behind the middle with sharper rays and rainbow streaks bursting out between the bars, plus softer lights behind each hump and a warm gold glow low on the right.
- The sky is brighter and dreamier, with wispy nebulae, sparkle dust spraying off the bar tops, and fine threads of light weaving behind the row.
