### Real artwork for every game

- Games from **Epic**, **GOG** and ones you add yourself now get real covers, backgrounds and logos instead of colour patterns. Marvel Rivals and Fortnite included.
- Oazy looks the game up on Steam by name, reads the art the Epic launcher already downloaded, and asks GOG Galaxy's game database, with Wikipedia as a last resort for covers.
- Everything is saved on your PC after the first download, so art shows instantly next time and Oazy doesn't keep asking the internet for games it couldn't find.
