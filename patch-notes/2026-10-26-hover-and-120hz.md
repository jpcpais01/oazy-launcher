### Steadier pointer, full refresh rate
- **Hover lets go properly**: moving the pointer from Oazy onto another app's window no longer leaves a button, card or taskbar icon lit up as if the pointer were still on it.
- **Oazy runs at your screen's full refresh rate (120 Hz, 144 Hz and up) everywhere the pointer is**: the taskbar, the desktop and menus no longer drop to 60 or 30 fps just because another app has focus. It only slows down when the pointer is somewhere else, to save power.
- If your graphics driver ends up pacing Oazy at half your refresh rate, Oazy notices and paces itself instead.
