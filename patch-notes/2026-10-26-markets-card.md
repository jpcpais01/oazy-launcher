### A cleaner Markets card
- **The first thing you follow leads** with a big price, the day's change and a full-width chart of the day.
- **Calmer rows** below it: the name over the ticker, a smoother sparkline, and the price over a quiet change chip.
- **Prices line up** digit for digit and no longer shuffle as they tick.
- **Open or Closed** shows next to the title, so you know whether the prices are live.
